About the Author / О Креаторе

Mara-Elizabeth Vest is a contemporary author and music creator specializing in psychological thrillers, dark suspense, and cinematic pop. Her literary work blends dark sci-fi elements, secrets, and human psychology. In addition to her writing, she creates atmospheric, narrative-driven dark pop music that complements the cinematic universes of her novels.

Пишу мрачные психологические триллеры на английском и создаю концептуальный dark pop саундтрек к своим книгам. Добро пожаловать в мою вселенную!
